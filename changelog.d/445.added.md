`python -m port.sim.draw <manifest>.toml` draws multi-slice samples from a version-3 manifest (`shared.unique` or mutation-tree clones, polygon layouts, Visium barcodes, Haldane phase switches) with every assumption stated in the TOML, from a per-gene normal baseline `port.sim.normal_fit` fits on CalicoST's normal spots. `[sample] realizations` redraws counts and phase over the same clones, and a 100 x 30 draw takes 6-9 s.
