`port.sim.draw` draws a spot's genes as a Dirichlet-multinomial about the normal baseline scaled by `(A + B) / 2`, its library growing with copies, and each (SNP, spot) independently from the `snp_spot_umi` law, with κ and the SNP dispersion fitted by `port.sim.normal_fit` on CalicoST's normal spots; `python -m port.sim.analysis plot` adds `plot_clones_genomic` over the true clone labels.
