Removed `port.extensions.outputs`' tables: a `run_cnaster_port` run no longer writes `cnv_states.tsv`, `cnv_segments.tsv`, `cnv_binlevel.tsv`, `clone_labels_integer.tsv`, `gene_segments.tsv` or `manifest.json`, nor rewrites `cnaster`'s `clone_labels.tsv`; their contents are in `cnamaste.h5`. Integer clones are scored by one rule, `outputs.integer_clones`, at 1.0 (`clone_ari_int`) and at 0.99 (`clone_ari_int_99`, a new ledger metric); `port.qa.scoring.integer_clones` is removed.
