# Recoloured clones of Swendsen-Wang samples against J (#751)

**TL;DR:** on 6 population members, SW samples of the run's clone-assignment field, each recoloured so every clone is connected, hold **3 clones above UMI50 at every J from 0.4 to 3.0**. 94–100% of those clones lie within one planted clone, and together they hold 81–94% of the spots; 4 clones are planted, the normal included. What J changes is the fragments. The median recoloured clone is about 10^3.6 UMIs up to J = 1.6 and crosses UMI50 (10^5.87) between J = 2.0 and 2.4. For a clone start, a sample at any of these J, recoloured and filtered at UMI50, gives the large clones; from J = 2 there are few fragments to discard.

- **Run:** `run_study --recoloured-clones run --seeds 0:6 --workers 3`, then `report`, on branch `claude/751-recoloured-clones` at sal `ef2fd06`; population members 0–5 (`population.toml`), each on the run's own field at the planted clones (`port.studies.stage.at_clone_assignment`).
- **Sampler:** `sal`'s heat-bath Swendsen-Wang at temperature 1 with the compiled cluster pass. A pilot's energy trace sets τ (Sokal), and the pilot grows four-fold until its kept half spans 50τ or the 6,000-sweep cap stops it. Burn-in is max(10τ, half the pilot) and samples are thinned at 2τ, 20 per chain.
- **Unresolved chains:** all 6 chains at each J ≤ 0.8 resolve. From J = 1.6, 41 of 42 chains are unresolved under the cap: their τ (21–94 sweeps as measured) is a lower bound. Longer pilots read τ = 83 and 225 at J = 1.6, so samples there may carry the start. The near-transition rows are the least certain.
- **Wolff** is not used. On member 0 a Wolff chain at J = 0.4 read τ ≥ 385 single-cluster steps, unresolved after 6,400, in 574 s, and two chains at J = 1.0 ran 40 min each without finishing. Both moves leave one law invariant, so Wolff would only have checked SW's mixing.
- **UMI50:** 10^5.87 [5.55, 6.09], at J = 1 from the limited population rerun (`population_summary_limited.json`, #745).
